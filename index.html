Domain Context: What Outliers Mean in Kerala Tourism

In this data mining study, an outlier is an unusual observation or significant trend deviation, NOT a data collection error. Tourism arrivals in Kerala are strongly influenced by the South-West Monsoon, backwater geography, mountain topography (Western Ghats), and major socio-cultural events:

2018 Floods

Torrential rain collapse in Alappuzha & Idukki (-85% drop).

COVID-19 Shock

Extended zero foreign arrivals and severe domestic lockdowns.

Festive Super-Surges

Thrissur Pooram & Onam peaks exceeding 2.4× baseline.

Key Project Findings

  • Mutual Consensus on 2019 Ernakulam: Both Isolation Forest and Local Outlier Factor simultaneously flagged Ernakulam in 2019 (4.58M total tourists) as the single highest confidence anomaly in Kerala's modern tourism history.
  • Algorithm Complementarity: Isolation Forest effectively isolated macro-scale volume spikes (Ernakulam 2022-2023 domestic surges), while Local Outlier Factor successfully isolated density variations in Alappuzha during the 2017-2019 flood-rebuilding cycle.
  • 91.43% Baseline Agreement: 64 out of 70 district-year observations showed unified agreement as inliers between tree-partitioning and density-clustering paradigms.

Model Comparison: Isolation Forest vs. Local Outlier Factor

Evaluation methodology: Because this is an unsupervised task without ground-truth labels, supervised metrics (Precision, Recall, F1) are invalid. We evaluate algorithmic concordance, Jaccard overlap, and contingency distribution.
