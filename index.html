Problem Statement & Methodology

Kerala tourism experiences intense spatial and temporal volatility influenced by tropical monsoons, hill station climates, backwaters, and cultural festivals. This platform implements a multi-technique anomaly detection architecture using Z-Score, IQR (Interquartile Range), Isolation Forest, and Local Outlier Factor (LOF) to separate true trend anomalies from standard seasonal cycles.

2018 Flood Shocks

Severe rain collapse in Alappuzha & Idukki (-85% drop) needing fast relief protocols.

Pandemic Disruptions

Extended near-zero international arrivals during 2020-2021 lockdowns.

Festive Super-Surges

Thrissur Pooram & Onam peaks exceeding 2.4× standard district baseline demand.

Key Project Findings

  • Consensus Outlier on 2019 Ernakulam: Both Isolation Forest and Local Outlier Factor simultaneously flagged Ernakulam in 2019 (4.58M total tourists) as the single highest confidence anomaly in Kerala's modern tourism history.
  • Algorithm Complementarity: Isolation Forest effectively isolated macro-scale volume spikes (Ernakulam 2022-2023 domestic surges), while Local Outlier Factor successfully isolated local density variations in Alappuzha during the 2017-2019 flood-rebuilding cycle.
  • 91.43% Baseline Agreement: 63 out of 70 district-year observations showed unified agreement as inliers between tree-partitioning and density-clustering paradigms.

Actionable Policy Insights & Strategic Recommendations

Converting detected outlier patterns into concrete policy, infrastructure, and crisis planning frameworks for the Department of Tourism, Government of Kerala.

1. Dynamic Demand Forecasting

  • Predictive Traffic Management: Forecast seasonal surges in Ernakulam (Kochi Biennale) and Thrissur (Pooram) to dynamically allocate KSRTC transit buses and parking lots.
  • Dynamic Hotel Tariff Caps: Implement algorithmic surge bounds during high-outlier festival windows to prevent price-gouging of domestic tourists.

2. Data-Driven Policy Planning

  • District Tourism Council (DTPC) Staffing: Allocate tourism police, lifeguards, and sanitation workers proportionately based on outlier-calibrated visitor volumes.
  • Tax & Subsidy Calibration: Provide off-season subsidy relief during heavy monsoon slumps (June-July) to keep houseboat operators afloat.

3. Rapid Crisis & Disaster Response

  • Automated Early Warning Signals: When footfalls drop >40% below seasonal median in highland districts (Munnar, Wayanad), automatically activate safety audits for landslides.
  • Emergency Evacuation Registries: Real-time digital tourist registration at hotels during red-alert monsoon months to track foreign & domestic guests.

4. Emerging Destination Development

  • Northern Circuit Infrastructure: Redirect marketing investments to Malabar & North Kerala (Kasaragod, Kannur, Wayanad) to de-congest southern circuits.
  • Niche Tourism Promotion: Promote monsoon Ayurveda wellness packages to turn off-season drops into high-revenue medical tourism periods.
